Terms

Inkcadence draws the words you type, in a hand learned from handwriting you provide. The software is free and licensed under MIT. These terms cover how you may use the pages it makes.

Only your own handwriting

The writing you photograph must be yours. You confirm that in the app before a hand is built. Do not upload someone else writing and present the result as yours.

You write the words

You supply every word. Inkcadence does not draft text for you.

Where a page must be written by hand

Do not submit an Inkcadence page where a person is required to write that page by hand. Do not use a page as a signature, an identity document, or any record that is supposed to come from a pen in the moment.

The AI-assisted note

Exports carry a note that the page was written with AI assistance. A footer on the page shows the same note and is on by default. Turning the footer off does not remove the note from the file.

The software

The app is provided as it is, without a warranty. It runs in your browser. There is no account and no payment.
