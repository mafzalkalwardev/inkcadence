How Inkcadence works

Assignment pages teach the look. Built-in styles (or an optional sheet) supply letterforms. The writer lays your text out on A4 paper.

Step 1

Assignment pages

Upload one to eight full written pages. Inkcadence measures slant, stroke, spacing, pen colour, and heading patterns.

Step 2

Match a hand

Auto-match one of ten built-in writing styles, or lock exam-neat / notes-normal yourself.

Step 3

Harvest + score

Letter crops are pulled from your pages. A style score and preview show what was learned.

Step 4

Style pack

Save the pack with your inks and matched hand. Built-ins stay in the sidebar for instant writing.

Step 5

Write

Paste assignment text (# for headings). Pick lined, blank, or grid A4 and a layout preset.

Step 6

Export

Download PNGs, download PDF, or print. Leave the AI watermark on when disclosure matters.

Questions and answers

These are the full answers. They match this repository as of 11 November 2026.

What is Inkcadence?

Inkcadence is an open-source app that learns a handwriting style from a person’s own pages and writes new pages in that style. It runs in the browser. This tree has no neural service.

Who is Inkcadence for?

Inkcadence is for people who want notes, letters, or practice pages in their own hand. It is not a tool for forging signatures, identity documents, certificates, or graded exams.

How does Inkcadence learn handwriting?

Full assignment pages teach the look: pen colours, slant, stroke width, line spacing, neatness, and heading weight. Inkcadence auto-matches one of ten built-in hands and harvests letter-like crops from your pages. An optional alphabet sheet can add exact glyphs if you want them.

What is a style pack?

A style pack is the saved profile for one handwriting style. It holds detected inks, stats, the matched built-in hand, harvested crops or sheet glyphs, and write defaults such as heading style and jitter. Built-in packs ship with the app; trained packs are yours.

What should I upload as sample pages?

Upload one to eight photos or scans of full written assignments — headings, numbered answers, body text. Flat lighting helps. Phone photos work; the harvest path is tuned for slightly dim or skewed pages.

Do I need an alphabet sheet?

No. The classic writer is assignment-first. The alphabet sheet is optional under an advanced disclosure if you want captured letterforms for every character.

What are the ten built-in writing styles?

They are ready hands that mix best (exam-neat) and normal (everyday) quality, with different heading treatments and ink colours. Auto-match picks the closest one from your page stats, or you can lock a style before you analyze.

What is the glyph sheet?

An optional printable grid. Write one character per cell, photograph it flat, and upload it. Inkcadence auto-aligns the grid. Most users can skip this and rely on built-ins plus harvested crops.

How many characters does the sheet capture?

The sheet covers lowercase and uppercase letters, digits, and the punctuation set used by the classical writer. A full sample run in this repository captured 80 of 80 letterforms.

What does the quality score mean?

The score is 0 to 100. It mixes glyph coverage, stroke confidence, whether pen colours were found, and how many sample pages you added. The sample walkthrough on this site scored 89 with 100 percent coverage. A low score means the classical writer has fewer real letterforms to stamp.

Can I preview a sentence before saving?

Yes. On the quality step, type a phrase and press Preview. Inkcadence renders that phrase with the draft pack so you can judge the hand before you save it.

How do I write a new page?

Open Write, pick a style pack or built-in, paste text (or press Format assignment), choose a layout preset and lined/blank/grid paper, then Render. Download PNGs, Download PDF, or Print.

What do writing size, variation, and seed change?

Writing size scales the letterforms. Natural variation adds jitter so repeated letters are not identical. Page seed picks a repeatable random stream, so the same seed recreates the same jitter.

How do I print or save a PDF?

Render the pages, then press Download PDF for a multi-page A4 file, or Print for the browser dialog. You can also download PNG files and export or import a style pack without raw photos.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photos are analysed in the browser and are not uploaded.

Where do accounts and style packs live?

In this browser, in IndexedDB with a localStorage fallback. This tree has no account server.

What is the difference between classical and neural writing?

This tree writes by stamping captured letterforms. The old neural service is archived on the experimental/backend branch.

What happens if the neural service is down?

There is no neural service in this tree. Writing uses captured letterforms in the browser.

Can I self-host Inkcadence?

The site is static and runs in the browser. The old API, database, and neural service are archived on the experimental/backend branch and are not part of this tree.

Is there a hosted plan?

No. This tree has no accounts or payments. The site is free and runs in the browser.

How much does Inkcadence cost?

The project is MIT licensed and the browser app is free.

Does the GitHub Pages demo need a server?

No. The classic writer and the Vite app both run in the browser.

Can I use Inkcadence offline?

After the page has loaded, writing does not need a network. The first visit downloads the app.

What is the AI watermark?

The watermark is a small disclosure on the rendered page that the handwriting was assisted. Leave it on when a reader might think the page was written by hand without software.

Can I use pages for signatures, exams, or official documents?

No. Do not use Inkcadence to forge signatures, identity documents, certificates, or work that is supposed to be written under exam conditions. The project documents this limit in the app’s privacy and terms.

Which browsers work?

Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari work. The app needs canvas, IndexedDB or localStorage, and modern JavaScript. A keyboard and a way to upload images are required to train a pack.

Which image formats can I train on?

Use any image type your browser can decode, typically JPEG, PNG, or WebP. Photograph the page flat, with even light, so the grid and the ink are readable.

Can I keep more than one handwriting style?

Yes. Each style is a separate pack in the sidebar. Select the pack you want before you render. You can delete a pack from its row.

How do I delete a style pack?

In the app sidebar, use the delete control on that pack’s row and confirm. Deleting removes the pack from this browser.

Does Inkcadence detect pen colour?

Yes. Sample pages are clustered into pen and faint colours. The Write tab shows those inks as swatches, and you pick the one to render with. Highlighter-like bright strokes are treated as less reliable ink.

What paper styles can I render?

Lined A4 (assignment lines), blank A4, and grid A4. Layout presets set paper, margins, headings, and page numbers. Heading treatment can be underline, double underline, or none.

Can I share a style pack?

Yes. Export a pack file from Write. Photos are stripped so you share the hand profile, not the raw assignment scans. Import works offline in the same browser app.

Is Inkcadence open source?

Yes. The license is MIT. This repository is the static browser app. The old API and ML service are archived on the experimental/backend branch.

What does continuous integration check?

On every pull request and push to main, GitHub Actions installs dependencies, checks syntax, runs the tests, checks that the engine copies match, and builds the Vite app. The Pages workflow tests again, then publishes this site and the app.

How is the live site published?

The Pages workflow assembles this landing page, the screenshots, the Vite app at /app/, and the classic writer at /classic/, then deploys them with GitHub Pages.

Does the neural service train a large foundation model?

This tree does not ship a neural service. The archived experiment is on the experimental/backend branch.

Are source photos stored on the server?

This tree has no server. Photos stay in the browser.

How are passwords stored?

This tree has no API. A local account, when the app asks for one, stays in this browser.

Will the result look like a normal font?

No. A font repeats the same outline. Inkcadence stamps your captured glyphs and varies position and size with jitter, so repeats are uneven. Characters you left blank on the sheet fall back to a simple drawn form, which is why the quality score matters.

What are the limits of classical synthesis?

Classical writing can only reproduce characters it captured, plus a plain fallback for gaps. It does not invent a new cursive connection the way a large generative model might. Joined-up handwriting, messy photos, and empty cells all lower fidelity. The score and the preview are there so you can see that before you rely on a pack.

Does the app have a light and a dark theme?

Yes. Use Dark theme or Light theme in the app, or the control on this page. The choice is saved in this browser and shared with the app on the same site. The rendered paper stays paper-coloured in both themes.
